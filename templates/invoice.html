BILLING NOTES
How charges accrue:
Storage is billed per megabyte per second based on the tier the file currently occupies. When a file is accessed (downloaded), it is promoted back to HOT and billed at the HOT rate from that point forward. Charges are recalculated every 10 seconds by the billing engine.
Tier transition policy:
HOT → COOL after 2 min inactivity
COOL → COLD after 5 min inactivity
COLD → ARCHIVE after 10 min inactivity
Any tier → HOT immediately on access

Thresholds are configurable in tiering_engine.py.
